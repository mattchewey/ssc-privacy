Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Sudo Supply Co. ("we", "us", or "our") operates sudosupplyco.com and associated social media accounts. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as when you make a purchase, contact us, or message us on social media. This may include:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your shipping address and phone number (if provided)
  • Order details, such as items purchased and order history
  • The contents of messages you send us

When you visit our website, we may also automatically collect basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited.

We do not sell your personal information.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Process, fulfill, and ship your orders
  • Respond to questions and provide customer support
  • Send order confirmations and shipping updates
  • Improve our products, website, and services
  • Prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations

Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working and to understand how visitors use it. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some parts of the site may not work properly without them.

Payment Processing

Payments are handled by our sales platforms and their payment processors. We never see or store your full credit card or payment details.

Third Party Services

We share information only as needed to run our business, with:

  • Etsy, which hosts our shop and processes orders and payments
  • Printify and its print partners, which produce and ship your order
  • Shipping carriers, which deliver your order

These services have their own privacy policies, which govern how they handle your information. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Social Media

If you follow, message, or interact with us on social media, we can see the information you make available to us on that platform, such as your username and the messages you send. That platform's own privacy policy also applies.

Marketing Emails

We only send marketing emails if you have opted in. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email or by contacting us.

Data Retention

We keep order records for as long as needed to fulfill orders, provide support, and meet tax, accounting, and legal requirements. We keep other information only as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this policy.

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct information that is inaccurate
  • Request that we delete your information
  • Opt out of marketing communications

Residents of California (CCPA/CPRA), the European Economic Area, and the United Kingdom (GDPR) have these rights under law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising them.

Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of your data, including data received through our social media accounts or apps, email info@sudosupplyco.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and include the email address or username associated with your account or order. We will confirm your request and respond within 30 days. We may need to keep certain records, such as order and tax records, where required by law.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our shop is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated version on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
